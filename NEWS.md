# PORTO 0.1.0

* Initial source-package release of four ordinal DEA/BoD functions.
* Single automatic unit input/output, free VRS intercept and pooled tie masses.
* Default weak preferences and exact strict surrogate shares.
* Optional `Gamma` for the nested moderate preference family.
* Undefined radial virtual shares are reported as `NA`; nonoptimal LP statuses
  are returned with a warning.
